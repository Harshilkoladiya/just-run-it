# CapitalScale: Grow Your Wealth

Create a premium, responsive multi-page financial education and investment platform named “CapitalScale”.

Purpose:

CapitalScale helps beginners and young professionals learn about real estate investing, personal finance, SIPs, mutual funds, stocks, wealth-building, and digital investment tools. It is an educational digital platform, not a financial advisory service. Include clear “for educational purposes only” risk disclaimers and never promise returns.

Design direction:

Use the attached reference template as visual inspiration only. Create a polished dark-mode-first site with:

- Black / near-black background

- White typography, electric blue accents, subtle blue gradients and glows

- Modern Geist-style sans-serif typography; monospace labels for small tags and navigation details

- Minimal, premium fintech aesthetic

- Rounded cards, thin dark borders, clean grid layouts

- Large editorial hero headings, subtle animations, smooth scroll effects, but respect reduced-motion preferences

- Fully responsive mobile navigation and layouts

- Use high-quality finance / city / real-estate imagery and simple chart-style visuals; do not copy any brand or text from the reference

Create these working routes/pages:

1. Home (`/`)

2. About Us (`/about`)

3. Learn / Content Hub (`/learn`)

4. Real Estate Investing (`/real-estate`)

5. SIP & Mutual Funds (`/mutual-funds`)

6. Personal Finance (`/personal-finance`)

7. Investment Calculators (`/calculators`)

8. Resources / Blog (`/resources`)

9. Contact Us (`/contact`)

10. Disclaimer & Privacy (`/disclaimer`)

Shared site elements:

- Sticky header with CapitalScale logo and nav links: Home, Learn, Investments, Calculators, Resources, About, Contact

- A prominent “Start Learning” button

- Mobile hamburger menu

- Footer with newsletter signup, quick links, social links, disclaimer, privacy link, and copyright

- All nav links and CTAs must work between pages

Home page:

- Hero headline: “Scale your financial future.”

- Subheading: “Clear, practical learning for investing in mutual funds, SIPs, real estate, and the digital economy.”

- CTAs: “Explore learning paths” and “Calculate your SIP”

- Elegant background image/video-style visual of a modern skyline or investment dashboard with a dark overlay

- Four investment category cards: Mutual Funds & SIPs, Real Estate, Personal Finance, Digital Investing

- “How CapitalScale works” 3-step section: Learn, Plan, Grow

- Featured learning articles

- Simple investment growth visual with illustrative data only, clearly labelled as an example

- Testimonials section with fictional placeholder testimonials

- FAQ accordion

- Final CTA section and risk disclaimer

About page:

- Mission: make financial learning simple, accessible, and practical

- Values: clarity, discipline, transparency, long-term thinking

- Team placeholder cards and a clean timeline of CapitalScale’s vision

Learn / Content Hub:

- Search field, categories, filters, featured guides, beginner-to-advanced learning paths

- Course/card topics including “SIP Basics,” “How Mutual Funds Work,” “Real Estate Fundamentals,” “Build an Emergency Fund,” and “Understanding Risk”

Real Estate page:

- Explain residential, commercial, REITs, rental yield, property research, down payment planning, and risks

- Include a rent-versus-buy comparison and beginner checklist

- Make all figures illustrative only

Mutual Funds & SIP page:

- SIP calculator with monthly contribution, estimated annual return, years, invested amount, estimated value

- Fund-type education cards: equity, debt, hybrid, index

- Explain expense ratio, risk level, diversification, and compounding

- Include a strong disclaimer that calculator results are estimates, not guaranteed returns

Personal Finance page:

- Budgeting, emergency funds, debt management, insurance basics, tax planning, and goal setting

- Add an interactive monthly budget planner UI

Calculators page:

- SIP calculator

- Lumpsum calculator

- Compound-interest calculator

- Emergency-fund calculator

- Use clean cards and clear result panels. Keep every output illustrative and show a disclaimer below each calculator.

Resources page:

- Blog/article card layout with categories, search, featured article, newsletter subscription form

- Create sample article titles and preview excerpts related to investing and financial literacy

Contact page:

- Contact form with name, email, topic, and message fields

- FAQ links and support email placeholder

- Validate the form and show a polished confirmation state

Technical requirements:

- Build a complete React website with reusable components and clean route-based navigation

- Use accessible semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly interactions, readable contrast, and proper form labels

- Add unique page titles and meta descriptions for each route

- Include loading, empty, and error states where relevant

- Do not use real-time market prices or make investment recommendations

- Use INR (₹) in all financial examples

- Create clean placeholder content that can easily be edited later
